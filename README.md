# Hi, I'm Juhan 👋

I'm an Economics graduate from UT Austin with a minor in Statistics & Data Science. I explore how I can use data to make better business decisions when budgets, uncertainty and human attention are limited.

I start my projects with a practical choice, compare alternatives and explain what my evidence supports. I am interested in analytics and AI consulting, and I am preparing for graduate study in business analytics.

## My projects

| Project | What I did | What I learned |
|---|---|---|
| [MLB Hitter Analysis — Signals for Next Season](https://github.com/younjuhan03/mlb-hitter-decision-analysis) | I compared next-season batting forecasts and tested a screening rule for limited scouting review slots. | I improved 2026 forecast error with current skill measurements, but my screening rule did not improve historical growth selection. |
| [RetailOS — Inventory Decisions](https://github.com/younjuhan03/retail-decision-intelligence) | I compared four ordering policies under a shared purchasing budget using historical retail sales. | I found a 0.51% modeled cost advantage for point-forecast optimization, but it won only 8 of 17 periods and had a lower fill rate. |
| [PriceLab — Experimental Pricing & Demand](https://github.com/younjuhan03/price-demand-experiment) | I reanalyzed a randomized bed-net pricing experiment and built a calculator connecting tested prices to acquisition targets. | I found similar receipt proxies at 20 and 40 KSh despite very different acquisition rates; I could not establish a reliably optimal price. |
| [Complaint Review Assistant](https://github.com/younjuhan03/complaint-review-assistant) | I combined a text classifier with rules for leaving uncertain complaints to human review. | I deferred 77 of 140 errors while also sending 68 correct classifications for review, making the workload tradeoff explicit. |
| [AWAKE Fundraiser Planner](https://github.com/younjuhan03/awake-fundraiser-planner) | I used available fundraiser records to build production scenarios beyond confirmed pre-orders. | I found that extra items needed about 50.3% sell-through to cover their cost under my price and historical cost assumptions. |

I explain each project's question, my contribution, results and limits in its README. I link the underlying reports, calculations and setup instructions so my conclusions can be inspected.

## How I approach analysis

- I define the decision and a simple comparison before adding model complexity.
- I evaluate the resulting choice alongside prediction accuracy.
- I keep assumptions, uncertainty and unfavorable results visible.
- I use AI tools to support development, including code and documentation, and document the evidence behind my conclusions.

I want to deepen my understanding of forecasting, experimental design, optimization and decision evaluation, then apply those methods to choices I can explain, test and improve.
