# Hi, I'm Juhan 👋

I'm an Economics graduate from UT Austin with a minor in Statistics & Data Science. I explore how I can use data to make better business decisions when budgets, uncertainty and human attention are limited.

I recently organized my analytical work into this public portfolio. I start with a practical choice, compare alternatives and explain what my evidence supports.

## My featured projects

| Project | What I did | What I learned and want to deepen |
|---|---|---|
| [PriceLab — Experimental Pricing & Demand](https://github.com/younjuhan03/price-demand-experiment) | I reanalyzed a randomized bed-net pricing experiment and built a calculator connecting tested prices to acquisition targets. | I learned how acquisition targets change a price recommendation. I explain my question, contribution, results, limits and next validation step in each README. I link the underlying reports, calculations and setup instructions so my conclusions can be inspected.

## How I approach analysis

- I define the decision and a simple comparison before adding model complexity.
- I evaluate the resulting choice alongside prediction accuracy.
- I keep assumptions, uncertainty and unfavorable results visible.
- I use AI tools to support development, including code and documentation, and take responsibility for the analytical choices and interpretations I present.

## What I want to learn next

Across these projects, my recurring interest is making decisions under uncertainty. I have learned to compare analytical models; I now want to get better at translating their outputs into choices under real constraints and assessing whether those choices improve on existing practice.

Graduate study in business analytics would help me connect experimental design, optimization and decision evaluation through rigorous methods and collaborative work. As I work toward analytics and AI consulting, I want to bring that same discipline to AI tools: define the decision they support, identify where human judgment belongs and test whether the resulting workflow improves outcomes.
